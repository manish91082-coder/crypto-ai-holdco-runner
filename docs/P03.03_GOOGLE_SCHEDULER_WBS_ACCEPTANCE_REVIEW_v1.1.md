## 4. Replay and identity assessment
The replay guard implements at-most-once admission, not exactly-once processing. A new tick UUID alone does not prove a new bearer. The unique token-hash constraint will reject a second legitimate tick if Apps Script reuses the same still-valid ID token. Only separate real owner-controlled trigger executions can determine that behavior. Do not relax replay controls based solely on synthetic unit tests.

The scalar audience check now aligns with Code.gs preflight. Multi-audience tokens are denied before JWKS fetch. The subject hash binding remains exact. No raw token or subject may be logged or persisted.
